# Muze Taste-Label Banner Generation Brief

I need a consistent collection of 35 achievement banners for the taste labels in my Telegram bot, Muze.

These banners will first appear when a user earns a taste label by completing a favorites list. Each banner may later be reused for profiles, sharing, achievements, discussions, and promotional material.

## Locked visual rules

Apply these rules to every image:

- Exact canvas: 2172 × 724 pixels.
- Exact aspect ratio: 3:1 landscape.
- Cinematic editorial illustration with subtle Art Deco and celestial influences.
- Rich painted texture: sophisticated, atmospheric, slightly dreamlike, and premium.
- Muze palette: deep midnight navy and dark teal backgrounds, antique gold/copper linework, warm amber light, muted turquoise, and restrained burnt orange accents.
- Use fine golden orbital lines, small stars, particles, halos, arcs, or geometric circles as subtle connecting motifs.
- Each banner should tell a miniature visual story matching its label—not merely display one large emoji or object.
- Strong central or slightly off-center focal composition.
- Preserve some calm negative space so the image can later support interface overlays or localized text.
- Maintain good contrast and recognizability at Telegram/mobile size.
- Keep the same texture, rendering method, border treatment, lighting logic, and overall visual universe across all 35 images.
- Every banner must still have its own mood, composition, and distinguishing symbol.
- No written text, letters, numbers, captions, title, watermark, signature, UI, frame, or logo.
- No copyrighted characters, celebrities, book covers, movie posters, album covers, or recognizable intellectual property.
- Avoid photorealistic stock-photo appearance, childish clip art, emoji rendering, glossy 3D icons, loud neon cyberpunk, and generic AI collage.
- People may appear when appropriate, but use original anonymous figures with diverse, natural representation.
- Keep important subjects away from the extreme edges.
- Export each banner as a high-quality sRGB JPG and retain the highest-quality master available.
- Do not vary the aspect ratio or dimensions between images.

For continuity, choose one subtle recurring Muze signature—such as a tiny golden guiding star or glowing orb—and include it naturally in every banner. Once approved, lock it across the collection.

## File naming

Use the exact filename specified for every image. Do not change or translate filenames.
